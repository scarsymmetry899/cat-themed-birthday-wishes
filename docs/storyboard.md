Status: narrative map only. Scene implementation starts after character approval and the four-state Rive foundation test.

| Beat | Physical continuity | Copy | Scroll behavior |
|---|---|---|---|

| Finale | Photo develops; Marmalade looks, heart reaction, other cats/cake/confetti enter | “Happy Birthday, [Name].” | scrub reveal; party loop after upload |

## Camera language

The page is one stage, not stacked cards. Use pinned chapters, masked foreground wipes, camera-like lateral travel and offscreen handoffs. Marmalade always exits a beat in the direction required by the next. Typography can be bumped, pulled or revealed by props but never overlays his face. Desktop favors wide travel and negative space; mobile re-blocks every path so the cat stays dominant rather than scaling the desktop composition.

## Emotional pacing

Playful arrival → kinetic chase → warm reveal → comic jeopardy → quiet safety → personal recognition → celebration. The “You are deeply loved” and soft-landing beats receive the longest unanimated holds. Finale spectacle must not erase the intimate look toward the uploaded photo.
